Top 5 Failure Mode Clusters & Engineering Recommendations

Systematic Diagnosis

Detailed error taxonomy derived from analyzing golden evaluation misclassifications:

1

Confusing Account / Login with Other

Frequency: 8.0% of predictions

Short password lock/reset tweets like "can't login" are mapped to Other due to low lexical keyword count.

💡 Actionable Fix: Add deterministic keyword heuristic triggers for ("login", "locked out", "password") before zero-shot classification.
2

Over-Escalation on High-Confidence Queries

Frequency: 56.5% of total predictions

Strict retrieval similarity threshold (< 0.35) forces escalation even when classification confidence is high (> 0.85).

💡 Actionable Fix: Rebalance threshold weighting: allow high classifier confidence (> 0.80) to override low TF-IDF retrieval similarity.
3

Implicit Cancellations Mapped to General Inquiry

Frequency: 5.5% of predictions

Customer tweets stating "stopping my subscription" lack explicit billing words and get misclassified as inquiries.

💡 Actionable Fix: Expand taxonomy prompt definitions with implicit intent phrases ("quit", "leaving", "stopping renewal").
4

False Positive Keyword Triggers ("hack", "leak")

Frequency: 3.5% of predictions

Metaphorical tweets ("this song is a absolute hack") trigger security escalation flags.

💡 Actionable Fix: Refine sensitive keyword check using contextual n-grams ("hacked my account" vs single word "hack").
5

TF-IDF Retrieval Out-of-Vocabulary Outliers

Frequency: 4.0% of predictions

Slang or typo-heavy tweets fail n-gram overlap with standard historical resolution corpus.

💡 Actionable Fix: Upgrade retriever from pure TF-IDF to hybrid dense embeddings (e.g. `all-MiniLM-L6-v2`).
